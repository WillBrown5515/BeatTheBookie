Beat the Bookie is a football prediction game where you predict the final league positions of teams before the season begins. The closer your predictions are to the actual final standings, the more points you score.

Use the arrows next to each team to move them up or down and set your predicted finishing order. You can update your predictions up until the deadline, after which they will be locked.

For the main leaderboard, the full Premier League is scored, along with the top 6 teams from the Championship and La Liga. See how the scoring works.

You can also create custom mini-leagues with friends. Mini-leagues allow you to include more teams from each league in your scoring, so you can compare your predictions in more detail with the people you know.

You’re also competing against The Bookie, the official prediction account based on real betting odds. However, The Bookie finished 33rd out of 79 last season, so may not mount much of a challenge again!

Referral bonus: If you refer someone who is playing Beat the Bookie for the first time, you may be eligible for the referral bonus. If a player has returned from a previous season, their referrer will not receive the referral bonus.
